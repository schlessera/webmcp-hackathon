4 The locked frame — layout, line weight and palette

Layout is locked: header flows straight out of the status bar, the map runs edge to edge between two rules, only the brief scrolls. Three things define the line weight: outlines dropped from 2–2.5px near-black to 1.5px of a tinted dark at 45–55% opacity, so the frame still reads as drawn but stops fencing every element; hard offset shadows became 2px translucent ink instead of solid black; and the ruled-out dots lost their outlines entirely, since they carry no information worth a border. The drawn quality is intact — chunky radii, tilted stickers, heavy Bricolage display weights — it is the contrast that eased. Only the semantic borders (the active step-free row, the unverified row, the private row) stay full-strength in their own colour, so the outline now means something instead of being everywhere.

The composer is the locked one. “Also worth asking for” chips above the bar, and privacy chosen inline in the field before you speak rather than after. It is pinned rather than in flow, so it costs the map nothing: status bar 52, header 44, map 340, brief 244, composer 110 — the brief is the only part that scrolls.
